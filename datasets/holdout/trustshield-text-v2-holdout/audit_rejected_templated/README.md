# Rejected templated audits

These three JSONL audits were used by an earlier holdout construction attempt and are retired:

- `mendeley_financial_scams_audit.jsonl`
- `mendeley_financial_scams_ham150_audit.jsonl`
- `reportsmishing_imc25_audit.jsonl`

Their rationales were templated from message prefixes/suffixes plus stock category clauses. That made the old distinct-rationale count non-independent and did not provide a genuine content-grounded audit. They are preserved here for provenance only, are not trusted by the builder, and are listed with SHA-256 hashes and rejection reasons in `manifest.json`.
